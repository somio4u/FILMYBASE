**Problems still open:**

- **[major] Story doctor — Facts (Rules) and Sequence 8:** The rules state 'no ordinary person is allowed within 100 feet of the chariots'. If the crowd is 100 feet away, Babulu throwing his money 'slightly outside the inner cordon' lands the cash in a massive empty exclusion zone. The crowd wouldn't be able to reach it, let alone surge powerfully enough to force the NSG into a protective huddle over 100 scattered notes. *Fix:* Reduce the exclusion zone to 15-20 feet. Babulu (disguised as a servitor) throws the money directly over the heads of the outer police ring into the hyper-pressurized front rows of devotees. This causes a sudden, violent scramble that ripples into a crowd collapse against the barricades, triggering the NSG protocol.
- **[major] Story doctor — Sequence 1 and Facts:** Soumya drops the phone with auto-lock disabled, and the screen actively looping the murder video. If Babulu steals it 'Two hours into Rath Yatra', the phone screen would have been intensely glowing inside a bag for two hours, draining the 40% battery to zero and likely alerting the pilgrim. *Fix:* Tighten the timeline. Babulu must steal the phone within 15-20 minutes of Soumya dropping it in the cold open. This preserves the phone's battery life and the ticking-clock tension for the rest of the film.
- **[major] Story doctor — Sequence 1 / Cold Open:** DCP Rudra is a high-ranking, calculating syndicate boss who wears an impeccably ironed uniform. It is highly implausible and unnecessarily risky for him to personally physically tackle and stab a man in the middle of a massive morning crowd. *Fix:* Have a plainclothes hitman tackle and stab Soumya on Rudra's radio orders. Soumya's dying video still captures the hitman's face and links to the prior video of Rudra committing murder in the warehouse, keeping Rudra as the mastermind without making him a street brawler.
- **[major] Story doctor — Sequence 1 and 2:** In Seq 2, Rudra has completely shut down the mobile network via a fake bomb threat, meaning Babulu has no signal. Yet in Seq 1, Rudra successfully calls the phone. If the public network is dead, a standard phone call wouldn't connect. *Fix:* Clarify in Sequence 1 that Rudra uses a localized police IMSI catcher (Stingray) to force a closed-circuit connection to Babulu's phone. This perfectly explains why Rudra can call and track him, but Babulu cannot use WhatsApp or the internet to upload the video.
- **[blocker] Audience critic — Facts: world rules / Sequence 2:** DCP Rudra declaring a 'fake bomb threat' to shut down the internet would cause a massive, deadly stampede of a million people. Furthermore, the NSG would immediately evacuate the Chief Minister and cancel his visit, entirely breaking your climax. *Fix:* Change the excuse. Rudra initiates a 'localized cellular jamming protocol' around Bada Danda, citing standard 'anti-drone/VVIP security measures' for the CM's arrival. This believable administrative overreach achieves the blackout without causing panic.
- **[major] Audience critic — Sequence 8, Climax:** A phone placed in a pocket with its screen unlocked and playing will suffer accidental touches (pausing or closing the video). Also, the CM merely feeling a 'weight' might not prompt him to pull it out on live TV instantly. *Fix:* Babulu opens the video, cranks the volume to maximum, and hits play the exact second he slips it in. The CM hears his own pocket shouting the DCP's voice and a man screaming, forcing him to pull the phone out immediately in front of the cameras.
- **[major] Audience critic — Sequence 6 & 8, Mina's Arc:** Mina gets captured and spends the finale passively watching TV. For an active 'partner in crime', this is a frustrating diminishment of her agency. *Fix:* Mina shouldn't just be 'captured'. She intentionally gets herself 'caught' by making a massive scene near the media vans, drawing Rudra's ground spotters away to create the exact gap Babulu needs to breach the Pahandi crush.
- **[major] Odisha culture expert — Sequence 8: The Chhera Pahanra:** During the sacred Chhera Pahanra ritual, no politician (including the CM) is allowed near the chariot's inner cordon. Only the Gajapati Maharaja and specific priests are present. Placing the CM and armed NSG commandos in the inner circle during this exact moment is highly inaccurate and would deeply offend Odia devotees. *Fix:* Place the CM on the official, elevated VVIP viewing gallery set slightly back from the chariots. Babulu throws the money at the barricades below the gallery, causing a surge that forces the NSG to huddle the CM on the balcony just as the heavy Aarti smoke from the adjacent chariot drifts over them.
- **[major] Odisha culture expert — Sequence 2 / Facts sheet:** A city-level DCP cannot unilaterally order a city-wide mobile internet blackout. In India, telecom shutdowns require authorization from the State Home Secretary. The Chief Minister would instantly know the order was fake. *Fix:* Instead of a blackout, have DCP Rudra deploy localized military-grade signal jammers (commonly used by VIP convoys in India) around Bada Danda. He justifies this to the CM's security detail as a 'proactive anti-IED VIP protocol', creating a localized dead zone without needing state permission.

## 1. The promise

**Logline:** When a cynical teenage pickpocket steals a phone containing video evidence of a murder, he must outsmart the city's corrupt police chief who is using the massive Rath Yatra security grid to hunt him down.

**The question that keeps people watching:** Can a street thief outwit the chief of police in a crowd of a million people when the hunter controls the exits?

**What the audience is paying for:** A claustrophobic, high-stakes cat-and-mouse chase thriller amidst the sensory overload of a massive festival.

**Why "wow":** The villain controls the cameras, the cops, and the exits, forcing the hero to use analog street magic to survive in plain sight.

## 2. The hidden truth

DCP Rudra Pratap, the Chief of Puri Police, has been running a massive, illegal VIP-darshan and extortion syndicate, skimming crores of rupees from temple security funds. A junior temple servitor (Sevayat) named Soumya uncovered the paper trail and recorded Rudra executing a rival syndicate boss to consolidate power. Realizing Soumya had the footage, Rudra ordered his men to hunt him down in the alleyways of Puri just as the Rath Yatra festival began. Cornered, Soumya dropped the phone into a pilgrim's bag seconds before Rudra murdered him, making it look like a crowd-crush accident.

- **Six months ago:** DCP Rudra takes charge of Puri and sets up an underground extortion ring disguised as VIP security protocols.
- **Three days before Rath Yatra:** Soumya, an honest junior Sevayat, discovers the hidden ledgers and secretly records Rudra murdering a dissenting local politician in a warehouse.
- **Night before Rath Yatra:** Rudra learns of the leak and dispatches his plainclothes strike team to eliminate Soumya before the Chief Minister arrives.
- **Morning of Rath Yatra (Cold Open):** Soumya is chased to the edge of the early morning gathering crowd on Bada Danda, records a final dying declaration linking the previous murder video, covertly slips the phone into a standard middle-class pilgrim's bag just as Rudra tackles him from behind, making the stabbing look like a sudden medical collapse in the dense throng.
- **Two hours into Rath Yatra:** Babulu, working the crowd on Bada Danda, pickpockets the pilgrim's bag, acquiring the phone.

**Who knows what at the start:**

- **DCP Rudra Pratap:** He killed Soumya but the phone is missing. He knows the phone's MAC address and uses the festival watchtowers and IMSI catchers to track it.
- **Babulu:** Discovers the murder video after stealing the phone, realizing the voice booming over the festival loudspeakers belongs to the killer.
- **Gouri (The Pilgrim):** Nothing. She doesn't even realize a bleeding man dropped a phone in her bag, nor that Babulu stole it later.
- **Inspector Das:** Knows Rudra killed Soumya, but believes it was a necessary evil for 'maintaining order'. He does not know the full extent of the extortion.

## 3. Characters

### Babulu (17) — Hero
*Hero / Pickpocket. Wiry, sun-burnt, wearing a faded oversized t-shirt to hide stolen goods and a stolen neon tourist cap, quick-eyed and constantly scanning.*

- **Wants:** To save enough stolen money to leave Puri and start a clean life in Mumbai.
- **Needs:** To realize that running away solves nothing, and that he has a responsibility to the city that raised him.
- **Secret:** He has a hidden stash of 50,000 rupees (his escape fund) buried under a loose brick in the Markandeswar temple tank.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes everyone is corrupt and looking out only for themselves, so he should do the same.
- **Changes the plot when:** Decides not to smash the phone and run, but to answer Rudra's call and engage in a game of cat-and-mouse.
- **Arc:** From a selfish, cynical thief who preys on the crowd, to a localized hero who sacrifices his own freedom and wealth to protect the innocent.

### DCP Rudra Pratap (45) — Antagonist
*Villain. Impeccably ironed Khaki uniform, aviator sunglasses, imposing posture, always holding a walkie-talkie.*

- **Wants:** To retrieve the phone and eliminate Babulu before the Chief Minister's security detail leaves.
- **Needs:** Total control and submission from the city.
- **Secret:** He despises the religious fervor of the festival and views the million devotees purely as cattle to be managed and monetized.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes his badge makes him untouchable and that street kids are invisible and powerless.
- **Changes the plot when:** Uses facial recognition on the CCTV grid to lock down Babulu's neighborhood, escalating the threat from personal to communal.
- **Arc:** From absolute authority commanding the city from above, to public humiliation and arrest at ground level.

### Mina (16) — His partner in crime and closest friend
*Ally / Decoy. Dressed in a bright, cheap Salwar suit, wears fake silver anklets, incredibly fast hands.*

- **Wants:** To keep Babulu safe and stick to their usual small-time thievery.
- **Needs:** To trust that they can punch above their weight and fight the system.
- **Secret:** She deliberately botches some pickpockets if the victim looks genuinely poor.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes survival is the only victory poor people can achieve.
- **Changes the plot when:** Uses her network of street urchins to create a ground-level shell game, passing the phone invisibly hand-to-hand to keep it moving off the CCTV grid.
- **Arc:** Transforms from a frightened survivor into a brave operative who risks her life to breach the VIP cordon.

### Baya Mamu (60) — His mentor, retired pickpocket
*Mentor. Leathery skin, missing three fingers on his left hand, wears a traditional white dhoti and gamucha.*

- **Wants:** To live out his days in peace by the beach.
- **Needs:** To pass on his ultimate 'phantom hand' technique for a righteous cause.
- **Secret:** He once tried to rob a politician and lost his fingers as punishment from corrupt cops.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes the system is too big to fall.
- **Changes the plot when:** Explains the exact security blind spots created by the thick Aarti (camphor) smoke and the NSG's protective huddle protocol during a crowd surge, giving Babulu the blueprint for the climax.
- **Arc:** Moves from passive cynicism to actively running interference against Inspector Das to buy Babulu time.

### Inspector Das (35) — Secondary Antagonist
*Henchman. Sweaty, perpetually stressed, plainclothes cop wearing a checkered shirt and jeans, hiding a service weapon.*

- **Wants:** To impress Rudra and secure his own promotion.
- **Needs:** To confront his dwindling morality.
- **Secret:** He has kept the bloody knife used to kill Soumya, hidden in his locker as insurance against Rudra.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes following orders absolves him of guilt.
- **Changes the plot when:** Corners Babulu in the VVIP viewing dais but hesitates for a split second, allowing Babulu to escape.
- **Arc:** Goes from a loyal attack dog to a broken man who ultimately surrenders when Rudra falls.

### Chief Minister Anant Mohanty (55) — The Target
*The VIP / Unwitting Savior. Crisp white Kurta, surrounded by NSG commandos, polite but exhausted.*

- **Wants:** A flawless, highly publicized photo-op at the Rath Yatra.
- **Needs:** To realize the rot in his own state police.
- **Secret:** He is planning a massive anti-corruption purge but lacks the undeniable proof to arrest high-ranking officers.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes his security detail tells him the truth about the city.
- **Changes the plot when:** Reaches into his pocket on live national television to find the phone, playing the video immediately due to Babulu's setup.
- **Arc:** Remains functionally the same, but his presence acts as the ultimate immovable object that Rudra cannot control.

**The villain's plan:** DCP Rudra plans to use the state-of-the-art Rath Yatra security grid (drones, watchtowers, CCTV, and mobile IMSI catchers) to track the stolen phone's signal. Because he orders a complete mobile internet blackout across Bada Danda claiming a 'bomb threat', he knows Babulu cannot send the video online. Rudra deploys plainclothes strike teams into the crowd, intending to quietly assassinate Babulu, retrieve the phone, and dispose of the body in the chaotic crush of a million devotees, writing it off as a stampede casualty. He believes he is right because he views his extortion as a 'tax' for managing an unmanageable city, and considers street thieves like Babulu a disease.

## 4. Facts sheet

**Jobs and money:**

- Pickpockets operate entirely in cash; Babulu's life savings (50,000 rupees) are entirely in 500-rupee notes.
- DCP Rudra has off-the-books bank accounts but uses untraceable temple donation cash to pay his hitmen.
- The local street vendors pay a protection fee to the police, which Babulu exploits to blend in behind their stalls.

**Health:**

- The extreme July humidity of Puri causes rapid dehydration; running through the dense crowd exhausts stamina quickly.
- Soumya (the victim) died from a ruptured femoral artery; the blood on the phone case matches his DNA.
- Baya Mamu's missing fingers mean he cannot execute complex sleight of hand anymore, forcing Babulu to do it.

**Belongings and vehicles:**

- The stolen phone has an encrypted screen but Babulu bypassed it because Soumya turned off auto-lock while recording his death.
- The phone battery starts at 40% and ticks down in real-time throughout the movie, adding a relentless deadline.
- Police vehicles cannot move through Bada Danda due to the million-person crowd; Rudra's men must pursue on foot.

**Places:**

- Bada Danda: The three-kilometer Grand Road where the chariots are pulled. A sea of a million people, claustrophobic and loud.
- The Watchtowers: Elevated police platforms with sniper views and CCTV feeds, making stealth incredibly difficult.
- The VVIP Dais: An elevated, heavily fortified viewing stage next to the chariot cordon during Chhera Pahanra, guarded by NSG commandos.

**Rules of this world:**

- There is no supernatural magic; survival depends entirely on analog street smarts, crowd physics, and physical sleight of hand.
- Mobile networks and internet are completely shut down across Bada Danda because DCP Rudra declared a fake 'bomb threat' to lock the grid, meaning the video cannot be uploaded anywhere in the city.
- During the Chhera Pahanra (chariot sweeping ritual), no ordinary person is allowed within 100 feet of the chariots, making physical approach to the Chief Minister seemingly impossible without a massive diversion.

## 5. The climax (designed first)

**Where/when:** The heavily guarded VVIP viewing dais and inner cordon next to Nandighosa (Lord Jagannath's chariot) on Bada Danda, during the highly televised Chhera Pahanra ritual.

**The reversal:** The audience and the police expect Babulu to try and escape the blackout zone to upload the video to the internet. Instead, he deliberately runs into the most heavily guarded zone in Puri, using his thieving skills not to steal, but to plant the phone directly onto the Chief Minister.

**The hero's choice:** Babulu chooses to sacrifice his hidden escape fund of 50,000 rupees, tossing the cash slightly outside the inner cordon to create a frantic crowd surge that forces the NSG into a tight protective huddle over the CM.

**The cost:** He permanently loses his ticket to a clean life in Mumbai, trapping himself in the chaotic, dangerous streets of Puri forever.

**Payoffs that fire:**

- Babulu's hidden stash of 500-rupee notes.
- Baya Mamu's lesson on reverse-pickpocketing ('giving is magic').
- Soumya disabling the phone's auto-lock, keeping the screen alive despite the draining battery.
- Baya Mamu's blueprint of the VVIP blind spot when the massive Aarti smoke rises and the NSG huddles.
- Inspector Das's growing resentment toward Rudra, leading to a crucial hesitation.

**Final image:** Live on national television, the Chief Minister stares at the phone in his hand playing the murder video, while Babulu, penniless but smiling, vanishes like a ghost into the swirling ocean of a million devotees just as the massive chariot wheels finally begin to move.

**Why "wow":** DCP Rudra's inescapable, high-tech digital trap (drones, CCTV, internet blackout) is shattered by the oldest analog street trick in the world—a cash distraction, blinding holy smoke, and a flawless reverse-pickpocket in plain sight.

**After:** Babulu accepts Puri as his home, transitioning from a selfish thief to a local protector. Rudra is publicly arrested and disgraced. Inspector Das hands over the bloody knife as evidence to seal Rudra's fate. Mina takes over the street network as Babulu's equal, and the CM gets the undeniable proof needed for his state-wide anti-corruption purge.

## 7. Sequence blueprints

### Sequence 1: The Harvest on Bada Danda

- **Cold open:** A frantic POV shot of a bleeding man recording a video. He is chased to the edge of the early morning gathering crowd on Bada Danda. He covertly slips the glowing phone into the dense bag of a standard middle-class pilgrim just as a cop tackles him from behind, making the stabbing look like a sudden medical collapse in the throng.
- **Question:** Can a teenage street thief survive crossing paths with Puri's most dangerous man?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu works the dense Rath Yatra crowd, identifying the pilgrim, and flawlessly pickpockets the bag to steal the phone.
- **Villain's move:** DCP Rudra discovers the phone is missing from Soumya's body and immediately orders a lockdown of all Bada Danda exits under the guise of crowd control.
- **Clue (earned):** Babulu notices the phone case is smeared with fresh blood, earning the realization that this isn't a normal theft when he bypasses the screen to see the murder video.
- **False lead:** none
- **Hero learns:** The man shouting crowd control orders over the Bada Danda loudspeakers is the murderer on the video.
- **Audience learns:** Rudra controls the entire security apparatus of the festival and has a sniper's view of the crowd.
- **Set piece:** A rhythmic, high-tension pickpocketing montage synchronized with the deafening beats of the Ghantuas (traditional drummers) marking the start of the chariot procession.
- **Side story:** Mina is busy creating distractions for Babulu, unaware they are stepping into a murder plot.
- **Turn:** Babulu decides not to ditch the phone but to see what's on it, driven by his desperate need for leverage to fund his Mumbai escape.
- **Ending hook:** Babulu pulls out the stolen phone, discovering the screen is already awake and playing a bright, looping murder video. The phone rings; he answers, and Rudra, having pinged the IMSI sector, uses the CCTV AI to lock onto Babulu's distinctive neon tourist cap from a watchtower.
- **Plants:** Babulu's hidden stash of Rs 50,000 in 500-rupee notes, his sacred 'Mumbai escape fund' buried under a brick at the Markandeswar temple tank.

### Sequence 2: The Blackout Zone

- **Cold open:** Rudra's voice echoes through the earpiece, calmly promising Babulu that he will be dead before the chariot moves an inch. Babulu sprints into the suffocating crowd.
- **Question:** How does a thief escape a hunter who controls the sky and the cameras?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu actively evades the watchtower cameras by stealing a traditional Pipli umbrella from a vendor and navigating through the blind spots of the street stalls.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra activates the facial recognition software on the CCTV grid, turning every camera into a weapon, and deploys his plainclothes strike team.
- **Clue (earned):** Babulu tries to WhatsApp the video to a news channel but the signal fails, leading him to realize Rudra has shut down the entire Bada Danda mobile network under a fake 'bomb threat'.
- **False lead:** Babulu believes he can just run to the railway station, only to find heavily armed barricades blocking all exits.
- **Hero learns:** There is no digital escape; the internet is completely blacked out across the city.
- **Audience learns:** The phone battery is rapidly draining, creating a strict time limit before the evidence is lost.
- **Set piece:** A claustrophobic cat-and-mouse chase through a vibrant labyrinth of street vendors, with Babulu using his knowledge of protection-racket stalls to hide.
- **Side story:** Mina realizes Babulu is being hunted by cops, not rival thieves, and decides to intervene instead of laying low.
- **Turn:** Babulu realizes he cannot outrun the radio network; he must outsmart the physical grid.
- **Ending hook:** Mina gets snagged by one of Rudra's plainclothes hitmen in an alleyway, forcing Babulu to stop running and turn back.
- **Plants:** Soumya's phone has auto-lock disabled; the screen never sleeps, which Babulu complains is draining the battery rapidly from 40%.

### Sequence 3: The Matha Corridors

- **Cold open:** Babulu uses a stolen mirror to blind the hitman with sunlight, allowing Mina to break free, and they dive into the ancient temple monasteries.
- **Question:** How to outmaneuver an omnipresent enemy without relying on technology?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu seeks out his retired mentor, Baya Mamu, navigating the interconnected, unmapped roofs of the ancient Mathas to stay off CCTV.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra, frustrated by the camera blind spots, orders brutal interrogations of the local street urchins to smoke Babulu out.
- **Clue (earned):** Babulu studies the festival map on Baya Mamu's wall, identifying the VVIP viewing dais as the only place Rudra's men cannot freely enter with weapons.
- **False lead:** none
- **Hero learns:** The only way to expose Rudra is to physically hand the phone to someone with higher authority than the DCP.
- **Audience learns:** Baya Mamu lost his fingers to corrupt cops, establishing the extreme stakes of failing.
- **Set piece:** A stealth sequence across the ancient, crumbling rooftops of the Puri Mathas, contrasting the serene chants of monks with the deadly hunt outside.
- **Side story:** Rudra pressures Inspector Das to command the street sweeps, forcing Das to compromise his own morals.
- **Turn:** Babulu shifts from fleeing the city to planning an infiltration into the most heavily guarded zone on earth.
- **Ending hook:** Babulu realizes he can't just hand the phone over; he must reverse-pickpocket it into the Chief Minister's pocket.
- **Plants:** Baya Mamu demonstrates a 'put-pocket' trick, slipping a coin into Babulu's pocket unnoticed, stating that 'taking is greed, giving is magic'.

### Sequence 4: The Bloody Knife

- **Cold open:** Rudra publicly slaps Inspector Das in front of the precinct for losing Babulu, demanding he scour the slums until the boy is found.
- **Question:** Will the police hierarchy crack under Rudra's ruthless methods?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu and Mina organize the street kids into a ground-level shell game, passing the brightly glowing phone invisibly to confuse the CCTV tracking algorithms.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra realizes the phone is being passed around and orders his men to shoot the kids with rubber bullets to break the chain.
- **Clue (earned):** Babulu observes Inspector Das lingering at a barricade, noticing the hesitation in Das's eyes when ordering violence against the children, marking him as a weak link.
- **False lead:** Rudra tracks the phone's signal to a garbage bin, thinking they abandoned it, only to find it strapped to a stray temple cow.
- **Hero learns:** The police force isn't entirely loyal to Rudra; fear is the only thing holding his syndicate together.
- **Audience learns:** Inspector Das has the murder weapon hidden, giving him the power to destroy Rudra if he finds the courage.
- **Set piece:** The Shell Game: Five street kids in identical oversized shirts scatter through the dense crowd, constantly bumping into each other to pass the phone invisibly hand-to-hand, scrambling the facial recognition AI and splitting the hitmen.
- **Side story:** Inspector Das visits his locker, staring at his hidden insurance policy, contemplating mutiny.
- **Turn:** A hitman corners the youngest urchin; Babulu breaks cover to tackle the hitman, reclaiming the phone but exposing his neon cap to a drone camera.
- **Ending hook:** Babulu is identified by the drone; Rudra locks his coordinates and dispatches the entire strike team to Babulu's exact location.
- **Plants:** Inspector Das stares resentfully at the bloody knife in his locker after being publicly humiliated by Rudra, showing he isn't entirely loyal.

### Sequence 5: The Blueprint

- **Cold open:** Babulu narrowly dodges a police jeep that plows through a vendor cart, sprinting toward the Markandeswar temple tank.
- **Question:** How do you breach an impenetrable NSG cordon without a weapon?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu sneaks into the Markandeswar temple tank to retrieve his life savings, intending to use it not for escape, but as a tool for the heist.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra creates a choke point around the chariots by clearing out all non-essential personnel, tightening the net.
- **Clue (earned):** Baya Mamu explains the security geometry of Chhera Pahanra: when the Gajapati King arrives, priests swing massive Aarti (camphor) lamps creating thick smoke, and any external crowd surge will force the NSG into a blind, tight protective huddle around the CM.
- **False lead:** none
- **Hero learns:** There is a momentary window of physical contact during the huddle to cross the barricade.
- **Audience learns:** Babulu is risking his entire future (the Mumbai money) to finish this.
- **Set piece:** A tense, underwater evasion in the murky green waters of the Markandeswar tank, holding his breath as police flashlights scan the surface above.
- **Side story:** Mina decides to stay behind and create a decoy trail, knowing she might get arrested to buy Babulu time.
- **Turn:** Babulu secures his money and commits to the suicide mission of approaching the Chief Minister.
- **Ending hook:** The phone battery drops to 15 percent just as the deafening conch shells announce the arrival of the Chief Minister's convoy.
- **Plants:** Baya Mamu explains the security geometry of Chhera Pahanra: when the Gajapati King arrives, priests swing massive Aarti (camphor) lamps creating thick smoke, and any external crowd surge will force the NSG into a blind, tight protective huddle around the CM.

### Sequence 6: The Pahandi Crush

- **Cold open:** The deafening roar of the Pahandi begins as the heavy wooden deities are rhythmically carried to the chariots. Babulu uses the overwhelming crush of devotees pushing forward as physical cover to approach the inner circle.
- **Question:** Can Babulu survive the tightening net as the festival reaches its fever pitch?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu actively misdirects a pair of hitmen by planting a decoy phone on a known rival pickpocket, drawing the cops away.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra spots the decoy trick from the watchtower, deduces Babulu's true trajectory, and leaves the tower to personally lock down the inner VVIP ring.
- **Clue (earned):** Babulu uses the reflection on a tuba in the police band to spot plainclothes cops closing in on him from behind.
- **False lead:** Rudra assumes Babulu is trying to reach a satellite media van parked outside the blackout zone to broadcast the video.
- **Hero learns:** Rudra has abandoned his post and is now on the ground, making the threat immediate and physical.
- **Audience learns:** The blackout zone is absolute; if the phone doesn't reach the CM, the footage is useless.
