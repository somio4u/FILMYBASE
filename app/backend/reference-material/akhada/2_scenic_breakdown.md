# ACT 1 — THE START AND THE BREACH

## SCENE 1 — EXT. DEEP SPACE → SKY OVER PURI — DAWN
Black. Then stars — a slow field of galaxies turning like slow dust. For fifteen seconds only, the camera drifts toward one pale blue dot: Earth. No lecture, no long history — just the pull-in.

We punch through cloud and the noise of the world rushes up. A ship tears across the sky: **PARMARTH 1** — a lean courier craft, its hull scarred, blue plasma braid trailing from its vents. Inside, alone at the stick, sits **SAMIR**, young, still-faced, plugged into a neural harness that paints soft light across his eyes.

The ship banks over **future Puri** — a coastal mega-city stacked in rings of white stone and glass towers, temple spires rebuilt in shining alloy among them. And rising over it all, taller than any tower, a vast black-and-gold tower crowned with a slowly spinning ring of light: the **WIRELESS ORBITAL RECEIVER.** Beams of soft gold pour down from it into the city grid — and, as we watch, one beam **stutters and dies**, and a whole ring of the city goes dark for a heartbeat before the power crawls back. The city is sick. (No dialogue.)

## SCENE 2 — EXT. GRAND ROAD, PURI — MORNING
Street level. An ocean of pilgrims — a hundred million, the road a living river of bodies in white and saffron. Floating **holo-banners** drift above the crowd, and a calm **Council broadcast** (a woman's face, ten feet tall, made of light) plays over the square, giving us three facts as text and image bloom around her: the **energy crisis** (the failing grid), the **record crowd**, and that **Earth is the last protected holy place** in a galaxy where prayer is now a crime. Behind her image, three enormous chariots stand half-built by drone-swarms and human hands together. (Broadcast voice only; no character dialogue.)

## SCENE 3 — INT. HIDDEN PRAYER ROOM, OFF-WORLD COLONY — NIGHT
Hard cut. A cramped metal room on a grey mining world. A family kneels before a tiny hidden idol lit by a single flame. The door is **cut open** — not opened, *cut*, the metal glowing and folding — and **shadow-soldiers** pour in: man-shaped tears in the air, edges smoking black. One reaches for the praying father. It does not stab him. It **touches** him — and his skin greys, his eyes go to black glass, and he **rises as one of them.** The mother's scream cuts to silence as the flame gutters out. (This plants the zombify horror. No dialogue needed.)

## SCENE 4 — EXT. RECEIVER GANTRY, HIGH OVER PURI — MORNING
Wind and height. On a narrow gantry ringing the great Receiver, **RUDRA** (late 30s, controlled, careful eyes) walks a line of floating readout-panels with **OMM** (broad, warm, grease on his hands). Omm presses a palm to the tower's skin; under the metal, deep below the Grand Road, his earth-sense feels a **strain** — the panels show a soft red pulse he cannot name. He wants Rudra to hold the power back. Rudra pulls up the city grid — whole districts blinking dark — and, jaw tight, drags the Receiver's output slider **up**, past the yellow line, filing a calm approval note as he does it. The gold beams brighten. The city relights. A careful man, breaking the world by the book. (They speak — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 5 — INT. RECEIVER CONTROL NOOK — MORNING
A quiet alcove. Rudra taps a **holo-call** open. His **sister** appears, small and bright, standing somewhere down in the crowded city. Behind Rudra, for a flash, a memory-image ghosts in the glass: red Martian dust, a line of dead war-droids, a younger Rudra. He waves it away. He tells her (later dialogue) to stay safe and go home before dark; she teases him and refuses. He watches her image wink out, worried. (They speak — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 6 — INT. GLOBAL COUNCIL CHAMBER — DAY
A ring of floating seats around a slowly turning hologram of a blue-green exoplanet. **MEERA** (calm, exact) presents her terraforming plan — water-worlds blooming in fast-forward above the table. A councillor asks the hard question; without a blink she gives a **cold number** of colonies she would sacrifice to fund it. At the edge of the room, ignored, **TARA** (sharp, tired, a scanner-cuff on her wrist) throws up her own data: the sky over the temple is **folding**, dimension-lines stacking like cracks in glass. The Council waves it off as "receiver noise." Meera and Tara leave through the same door, already disagreeing. (They speak — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 7 — INT. PARMARTH 1 CARGO HOLD / STRATOSPHERE — DAY
Samir's ship, high over the coast. In the empty hold, one locked case floats in a stasis field: an **experimental navigation chip**, its surface crawling with tiny light. A handler's face flickers on the cockpit glass and gives the drop: Grand Road, mid-ritual. Samir only asks one thing (later dialogue) — when he gets paid. We see his screen route the fee the instant it lands: an automatic transfer to a **family name** he closes before it fully shows. His face gives nothing away. (They speak — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 8 — EXT. GRAND ROAD — PAHANDI — DAY (SONG SPOT A)
The great ritual. The deities are carried out of the Sri Mandir in the swaying, rocking **Pahandi** walk, servitors in white straining under them, the crowd's roar rising like the sea. Drums, conches, gold dust in the air. *(A short devotional Yatra song can live here.)* In the crush, the five brush past each other once, none of them looking: Samir dodges Omm's shoulder; Meera steps around Tara; Rudra's arm clips Samir. Five strangers, one frame, gone. (Song / no spoken dialogue.)

## SCENE 9 — EXT. RECEIVER + GRAND ROAD — DAY
Power use spikes with the swelling crowd. On the gantry, alarms bloom. Rudra, torn, pushes the Receiver to **full** — the crown-ring screaming white. The gold beams turn harsh. Above the temple, the sky begins to **ripple wrong**, like heat-haze over metal. Tara's scanner-cuff shrieks; Omm doubles over as the ground-strain spikes; in Parmarth 1, Samir's chip **glitches**, light spilling from its case. Three heroes, three places, one event felt three ways. (Minimal/no dialogue — reactions.)

## SCENE 10 — EXT. SKY OVER PURI — THE BREACH — DAY
The last unseen **kabacha** (sky-shield) gives with a sound like a snapping bell. The sky **tears** — a ragged black **portal** opening over the chariots. Cold pours out. And the **shadow army** comes down — hundreds of smoking man-shapes falling into the crowd, which turns from a festival into a stampede in seconds. Screaming, running, gold banners trampled. (No dialogue — chaos.)

## SCENE 11 — EXT. GRAND ROAD — THE RAKHYAKA — DAY
Against the tide of running people, one old man walks *forward*. **THE RAKHYAKA** — lean, ancient, a plain wooden **staff (danda)** wrapped in old silk in his hand. He plants himself before the front of the army and fights — and he moves like nothing in this century: no energy weapons, just the staff and his body, and shadow-soldiers burst apart at his touch. For a moment he holds them all. Then the army swells, too many, and a heavy dark strike catches him across the chest. He staggers. Blood. His breath goes ragged. He knows — he cannot win this. (Choreography-heavy; no dialogue.)

## SCENE 12 — EXT. GRAND ROAD — THE FREEZE — DAY
The Rakhyaka drops to one knee, plants the danda, and speaks an old spell (later dialogue). He **strikes the staff to the ground.** A ring of pale light rushes out — and **everything stops.** The falling banners hang. The screaming mouths freeze. The shadow army halts mid-lunge, the portal frozen mid-churn above. In the sudden, total silence, the wounded old man rises and **walks** through the frozen world. He finds them one by one, each frozen in a different place — Rudra on the gantry, Meera and Tara in the street, Omm mid-fall, Samir climbing from his landed ship. A **spark of white light** leaps from the danda to each of them — and the five are **torn out of frozen time.** Whiteout. (No dialogue.)

# ACT 2A — THE TIMELESS AKHADA

## SCENE 13 — INT./EXT. THE AKHADA — TIMELESS
The five slam onto warm stone, still screaming from a fight that is now gone. They are on a **floating akhada** — a round wrestling-ground of old stone and torchlight, hanging in an endless soft void, stars and slow galaxies drifting far below the edge. Around them rise the **BISWABASU CLAN** — dozens of the dead, in old cloth, calm and glowing faintly — who **bow** to the five by ancient names. The Rakhyaka, whole again here, no wound, tells them what they are (later dialogue): reborn protectors, the shield broken, the Lord's road nine days long. The five react in five ways — Samir backing toward the edge, Meera scanning for exits, Omm reaching to help a "person" who cannot be hurt, Tara already testing the air, refusing all of it. (They speak — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 14 — INT. THE AKHADA / MEMORY-LIGHT — TIMELESS (SHORT MONTAGE)
The Rakhyaka lifts the danda and the void fills with **living memory-light** — the origin, shown like a moving mural made of smoke and gold:
- The **Biswabasu** clan in a deep green jungle, worshipping Lord **Nila Madhava** by a hidden pool.
- **King Indradyumna**'s men taking the deity; the clan's grief; the two sides facing off; then the peace — the clan given the sacred service, the title **Daitapati.**
- The old gurus and tantriks foreseeing a **dark power** to come; the carving of the **danda from neem wood**; the building of this **timeless Akhada.**
- The cycle: a protector chosen, sent to Earth, guarding, dying, striking the danda so it **chooses the next.** Faces flick by across the ages — many protectors, one after another.
- Then the danda, now, shooting out **five** sparks instead of one — landing on our five. First time ever.
The memory-light hardens on one line, carved in the air (later dialogue): **the lathi serves only a heart that owes nothing; it kills the unworthy who touch it; only dark power can force it.** (Narration/dialogue later; visual montage.)

## SCENE 15 — INT. THE AKHADA — TIMELESS
Quieter. The five ask who they fight. The Rakhyaka's face closes. He tells them only a little (later dialogue) — that the enemy was **once one of them**, a chosen protector, and that **something happened to him** that turned him against the Lord. He will not say the name. The clan around them goes still and grim at the not-saying. (They speak — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 16 — INT./EXT. THE AKHADA — TRAINING — TIMELESS (MONTAGE)
Each hero is handed to a clan mentor — and, in a nice reveal, each mentor is that hero's own **student from a past life**, gentle with them. We cut between them:
- **OMM** cannot make the stone floor answer — it stays dead under his hands — until he stops shoving at it; and meanwhile he has cheerfully cooked for the whole clan twice, learning every name.
- **RUDRA** makes his palms **glow with heat** but clamps it before it becomes flame, sweating, holding it in.
- **MEERA** lifts a rope of clean **water** out of dry void air on her first try — and looks unsettled at how easy it was.
- **SAMIR** blurs across the ring on a cushion of **air**, too fast to turn, and shoots straight off the edge into open void before a mentor's wind snaps him back.
- **BISHU**, Tara's mentor, teaches her to open a **portal the width of a needle** — a bright pinhole in the air — and warns her (later dialogue) that the price is her hold on the present moment. (Montage; light dialogue later.)

## SCENE 17 — EXT. THE AKHADA RING — THE TRAINING FIGHT — TIMELESS
A real sparring bout, the clan ringed around roaring like a true akhada crowd. Five elements clash in pairs — fire hissing against water into steam, air throwing stone, Tara blinking short portals to dodge. Through the fight we READ them clearly: **Omm** shrugs off every hit, a wall no one cracks (underrated); **Samir** dazzles and then loses control and eats the floor (overrated); **Meera** moves clean and ends every exchange first (the best); **Rudra**, refusing to loose his fire, gets driven back and looks the **weakest** of them all — even though we've seen the furnace he's holding shut. (Choreography; light dialogue later.)

## SCENE 18 — EXT. THE AKHADA RING — THE FAILED JOINING — TIMELESS
The Rakhyaka lines the five up and explains the enemy's secret (later dialogue): he **eats** elemental force, so power thrown at him only feeds him — the only way is to **let force pass through.** They try to join their five powers into one. The streams fire at once — fire, water, stone, wind, light — and **collide** in a snarling knot that detonates. The blast throws them flat and **badly burns OMM**, who does not get up easily. In the smoke, we understand what they can't: five people each gripping their own guilt cannot let anything pass through them. Later, alone at the ring's edge, **TARA** has a silent **vision** — the danda in a hero's hand, the Rakhyaka gone — and tells no one. (Action + dialogue later.)

## SCENE 19 — INT. THE AKHADA — THE BREACH FROM WITHIN — TIMELESS
The clan freezes mid-task. Above the akhada, a thin seam of light hangs in the void — the **frozen-Earth portal, never fully closed.** Through it, something **drops** onto the sacred stone: a single **beast** — bigger than the shadow-soldiers, a hunched mass of smoking black with too many joints. It lands among the dead. The clan, who cannot truly die here, were never meant to face *this*. It lunges — and where it claws a clan member, the wound spreads **grey**. Panic in a place that has never known it. (Action; no/little dialogue.)

## SCENE 20 — INT. THE AKHADA — THE HARD CHOICE — TIMELESS
The five and the Rakhyaka corner the beast, but too late for one: a **beloved clan mentor** (one of their own past-life students) is turning — greying, eyes going to black glass, reaching for the others. The infection begins to spread down his arm to the ground. There is only one way to stop it. In a terrible, quiet beat, the heroes **put him down** — a first, real loss, and the clan watches its own child die in a world where nothing was supposed to die. The lesson lands like ice: the dark power can reach **even here.** If it takes the Akhada, the whole protector cycle — thousands of years — **ends forever.** They cannot wait to be ready. The clan blesses them. The Rakhyaka raises the danda and **strikes.** (Emotional; dialogue later.)

# INTERVAL BLOCK — THE GUARDIAN'S FALL

## SCENE 21 — EXT. GRAND ROAD, PURI — DAY (TIME RESUMES)
White light — and they are back, landing hard on the Grand Road in a tight ring, guards already up, still dressed in Akhada dust. For one held second everything is frozen around them — the falling banners, the screaming mouths, the mid-lunge army. They land right in front of the **RAKHYAKA**, who stands wounded but calm — and he **smiles** at them. Then time **snaps back on**: the roar returns, the blade falls, the world moves. The five turn as one to face the army. (No dialogue — a held hero beat.)

## SCENE 22 — EXT. GRAND ROAD — THE BATTLE + THE HORROR — DAY
The five cut loose for the first time on Earth — fire, water, stone, wind and light tearing through the shadow ranks in front of a hundred thousand witnesses, and the running crowd slows, turns, and begins to **chant** them on. Then the horror hits home: a shadow touches a fleeing pilgrim and the pilgrim **turns** — greys, rises, joins the enemy. All along the road, the front crowd — about **5000 souls**, the very people who would have pulled the three chariots — begin to change. The heroes freeze at the choice in their hands: these "demons" are the people. They will **not** cut them down. Their eyes go up to the **portal.** (Action; a shouted decision — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 23 — EXT. GRAND ROAD / SKY — THE LATHI PROBLEM — DAY
To shut the portal, the danda has to go up to it — but when Rudra grabs for the silk-wrapped staff, a jolt throws his hand off; **none of the five can hold it.** The Rakhyaka takes it himself. **SAMIR** wraps the old man in a spinning column of **air** and lifts him up off the road, rising toward the churning portal mouth while the others hold the ground. On the Rakhyaka's shouted order (later dialogue), the team **splits** — Meera, Omm and Rudra carve a path to the chariots to move **Lord Jagannath toward the Gundicha Temple**, finishing the sacred journey by force of arms. (Action; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 24 — EXT. SKY OVER GUNDICHA — THE FALL — DAY
At the portal's mouth, the shadow-soldiers **flow together** into one huge **devil-shaped monster**, a tower of smoke and teeth. It swats Samir's air-column aside and closes on the old man. The Rakhyaka fights it staff-to-claw in the sky — magnificent, doomed — until a killing dark blast punches through him. He **falls**, a long terrible drop, and lands broken on the stone **in front of the Gundicha Temple.** The **danda** is flung from his hand — it shoots *out* through the portal (leaving the tear **fully open**) and **spears into the ground** before the temple steps. As it strikes, the dying Rakhyaka rasps the last spell and a **dome of light** rises over the whole Gundicha Temple, sealing the Lord and the people inside. The monster breaks against it. (Action; a last spell — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 25 — EXT. GUNDICHA TEMPLE FRONT — THE LAST ORDER → INTERVAL — DAY
The five reach the fallen Rakhyaka under the humming dome. He is going. He tells them the truth (later dialogue): none of them can hold the lathi yet; the dome lives only while he lives; they must **take the Lord away and hide Him.** His hand falls. Outside the dome, the shadow army presses; the sky stays torn. Quick, grim, the five gather the Lord and, under cover of the dome's glare, **slip Him out and away** — unseen. On their five faces: a teacher dead, a war just begun.
**CUT TO BLACK. TITLE CARD: "The teacher is gone. The war just started." — INTERVAL.**

# AFTER THE INTERVAL — THE REVEAL AND THE HUNT

## SCENE 26 — INT. SRI MANDIR / EMPTY SANCTUM — DAY
Silence after storm. A tall armored figure walks the emptied temple, boots echoing. He wears a **faceless helmet** that seems to bend the eye away from him — hard to look at directly. He reaches the sanctum. He removes the helmet. **KALAPAHADA** — our first clear look at his face: old, beautiful, ruined, eyes full of a cold grief. He looks at the place where the Lord should be. **Empty.** (No dialogue yet — a reveal held in silence.)

## SCENE 27 — INT. SRI MANDIR — THE LATHI TAKEN + THE CLOCK — DAY
Kalapahada crosses to where the **danda** stands driven into the ground (the dome gone now, the Rakhyaka dead). He grips it — and it **fights him**, light crawling up the wood against his hand. He forces it, dark veins of his own power wrapping the staff, **chaining it** to his will. It obeys — but wrongly, unwillingly. He turns to the torn sky and speaks his taunt to the fled Lord (later dialogue): *the Yatra must be completed; you must come back; but I will not let you enter your temple; you will come to me.* Behind him the great portal hangs open like a wound. (He speaks — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 28 — EXT. SPACE / MANY WORLDS — FAME + THE ARMY — MONTAGE
Split-light montage. Galaxy-wide **broadcasts** replay the five tearing through the shadow army on the Grand Road — their faces on a thousand screens across a hundred worlds; colonies erupting in hope; leaders recording invitations ("come to us, bring the Lord"). Cut against it, darker: **Kalapahada's fleet** descending on world after world, his shadows sweeping cities, touching people who **rise greyed** — his army doubling, then doubling again. He is not chasing. He is **building**, and waiting. (Broadcast fragments; no character dialogue.)

# ACT 2B — THE JOURNEY

## SCENE 29 — EXT./INT. GRANTHALOKA — ARRIVAL & WELCOME — DAY (SONG SPOT B)
A new world: **Granthaloka**, all soft light and floating terraces, home of the galaxy's great library. Tara's world. The heroes' craft sets down among crowds who have waited generations to see the Lord in the open. A **welcome** blooms — lamps, flowers made of light, an old **father figure** (Tara's father) leading the rites; Tara hangs back, complicated, at the sight of him. The Lord is placed on a high lit dais. *(The devotional welcome song lives here — warm, the first safe breath in the film.)* (Song / dialogue later.)

## SCENE 30 — INT. THE DIGITAL LIBRARY — THE NAME — DAY
Tara's father leads them into the **library**: a vast dark hall with **no books** — only floating motes of light. When you name a text, its **keeper appears as a hologram.** They call the **MADALA PANJI** — and a tall figure of amber light rises, the living temple-chronicle. Around them the air fills with moving history: past ages, past attacks on the Lord, past protectors. And then the record speaks a name it has carried for centuries — **KALAPAHADA** — and shows his story: the chosen protector who suffered, who broke, who came to hate the Lord, who found a way to return with all his memory. The five stand inside the glowing history and understand, at last, exactly who hunts them. (They speak / narration — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 31 — INT. GRANTHALOKA — THE SCALE OF IT — DAY
An alarm-light. A distress feed floods the library: a **whole colony world gone dark**, then gone — Kalapahada's army sweeping it, the dead rising by the million. The heroes watch the scale of the thing they are running from. It is not one enemy. It is a growing tide. (Reaction; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 32 — EXT. GRANTHALOKA — THE ATTACK — DAY
The sky over Granthaloka tears. Kalapahada's forces fall on the library-world. This time the heroes do not flee first — they **stand and fight** to save the people, shielding the fleeing crowds, Omm raising stone walls, Meera drowning shadow-ranks, Rudra (still holding back his full fire) driving them off the terraces. A real, hard, **active** battle — the heroes as protectors, not runaways. (Action; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 33 — EXT./INT. GRANTHALOKA — THE FATHER'S FALL — DAY
The tide is too strong. **Tara's father** makes his stand not to cover a retreat but to protect the **heart of the library** — the living records, the memory of the Lord — sealing the archive with his own life as the shadows take him, pressing into Tara's hands one last thing: an old **palm-leaf verse** her family has copied for four centuries. The heroes fight *toward* him and reach him too late — they tried, and failed, and that is the difference. Grief-struck, they lift the Lord and pull back to the ship as Granthaloka burns. (Emotional; dialogue later — shaped to feel DIFFERENT from the Rakhyaka's fall.)

## SCENE 34 — INT. THE SHIP / OPEN SPACE — THE MIRROR — NIGHT
Quiet engine-hum. The five sit apart in the dark hold with the Lord's soft light between them. Now that they know Kalapahada's story, each privately sees a piece of himself in it — and we feel it in small silent images: Samir staring at the family transfer on his screen; Meera's hand hovering over a casualty count; Rudra flexing a hand that will not burn; Omm holding a weight he won't set down; Tara clutching the palm-leaf verse she doesn't believe. The villain is the end of the road each of them is on. (Mostly silent; light dialogue later.)

## SCENE 35 — EXT./INT. STOP 2 — THE SECRET SHOWN — DAY (SONG SPOT C, OPTIONAL)
A hidden faith-world, its people worshipping in secret caverns lit by bioluminescent water. A kind old **MAHA GURU** takes them in. When a scout-force of shadows attacks, the Guru does the impossible in plain sight: a killing blow comes, and he does **not** block — he **lets it pass through him**, bending like water, and it strikes nothing, dissolving away. And he **lives.** He turns and, calm, begins to teach them what they just saw (later dialogue). Something in **Meera** cracks open — she stops counting. *(A soft, emotional song can live around this stop.)* (Action + dialogue later.)

## SCENE 36 — EXT. DEEP SPACE / KALAPAHADA'S FLAGSHIP — THE LATHI TEST — DAY
The heroes make a hard choice: go at Kalapahada directly and take the lathi back. They ambush his flagship. Full force — Rudra finally loosing more of his fire than ever — everything they have. And he **eats all of it**, elemental power pouring into him and making him brighter, larger, the chained lathi flaring in his grip. He barely bothers to strike back. They flee, hulls smoking, shaken to the bone: fighting him only makes him **stronger.** (Big action; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 37 — INT. THE SHIP — ROCK BOTTOM — NIGHT
The lowest point. Omm bleeds through an old field-dressing; Samir won't meet anyone's eyes; the danda's absence sits like a hole. Tara's long-running Puri data finally resolves on her screen — and it is the worst possible truth: the **Receiver thinned the sky.** Kalapahada did not break the shield alone. **Rudra** opened the door — not with his temper, but with his *caution*, the safe, measured, approved decision. The team fractures in the dark. The clock is almost gone. (Heavy; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 38 — INT. THE SHIP / MEMORY-LIGHT — THE TURN — NIGHT
In the wreck of themselves, it finally lands. They replay the Guru: you can only let force pass through you if you are **not gripping your own guilt.** The lathi rule clicks — it serves only an open heart; Kalapahada holds it only by force, on borrowed time. One by one, in small, real beats, each hero sets a weight down (shown, not yet spoken). They piece together his whole plan — **the Brahma, the portal, the Samudra Manthan, immortality** — and understand there is only one place left to go: **back to Puri, for the last day.** They turn the ship for Earth. (Emotional turn; dialogue later.)

# ACT 3 — THE CLIMAX: SAMUDRA MANTHAN

## SCENE 39 — EXT. SPACE NEAR EARTH — THE TRAP SPRINGS — DAY
As they come in toward Earth, reality **buckles.** The stars smear; the ship is gone; the five are suddenly standing in a strange, beautiful **elsewhere** — and Kalapahada is there, waiting. He has pulled them into a **maya** — an illusion-world built to hold them. (No dialogue — a reality-shift.)

## SCENE 40 — EXT. THE MAYA (SHIFTING REALMS) — RUDRA IS FORGED — CONTINUOUS
A huge, gorgeous, wrong battle. The fight throws them across impossible places — a burning red world, a drowned one, a sky of falling temples — and every time they beat Kalapahada he simply **reforms**, feeding on their power. A loop with no exit. **TARA**, of all of them, feels the seams — *this is not real.* But knowing is not enough; they are still trapped, still swinging, still feeding him. Then the answer, made physical: the only way out is to **stop fighting and take the hard power.** The chained lathi hangs between them and Kalapahada, its killing light bared. **RUDRA** walks into it — arms open, ready to die for the others — and **grips the lathi's killing power.** It should destroy him. Instead, because his heart is finally open and owes nothing, it **pours into him** — fire he has hidden his whole life erupting free — and **transforms him into a warrior**, the lathi's silk burning away, the staff blazing and **his.** That true power **shatters the maya** like glass. (Huge action + transformation; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 41 — EXT. GRAND ROAD, IN FRONT OF THE SRI MANDIR — THE SEIZING — DAY
Reality snaps back. No festival, no chariots now — just the solemn **rituals**: the servitors bearing **Lord Jagannath** on foot back toward the Sri Mandir to be restored, the city holding its breath. The five, Rudra now blazing with the lathi, move with them. They are almost there — the temple rising just ahead — when **KALAPAHADA strikes**, dropping into the road **in front of the temple**, cutting the Lord off before He can reach the steps. In one brutal move he **seizes the idol** and rises with it, tearing upward and out over the coast toward the open sea. (Action; a taunt — dialogue later.)

## SCENE 42 — EXT. MID-SEA — THE BRAHMA & THE CHURNING — DAY
Far out over black water, Kalapahada holds the idol aloft and **draws the Brahma out** of it — a small, blinding point of pure light. The sky and sea answer: the **great portal** rips wide above the waves, and out of the water rise the **nagas** first — vast serpents of shadow and scale — and then the **danavas** pour through the sky-tear. Kalapahada begins the **dark Samudra Manthan**: a colossal serpent wound around a black mountain thrust up from the sea-floor, the ocean beginning to **turn**, to boil, poison-haze rising off it. He will churn up the amrit and drink himself immortal. (Vast VFX action; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 43 — EXT. MID-SEA — THE FINAL BATTLE — CONTINUOUS
The five arrive over the churning sea against everything at once — nagas, danavas, and Kalapahada at the center. But now they do not fight his way. They **yield.** Standing in a line on a shard of floating stone, they build the true technique: Omm's force flows **into** Meera's, into Samir's, into Rudra's — a single **thread**, all of it channeled through **TARA** as the living conduit, and **led by Rudra with the blazing lathi.** At the key second Tara **drifts** — her mind slipping toward the timelines, the thread fraying — until **OMM's** hand closes on her shoulder and pulls her back to the now. The thread holds. (Climactic action; dialogue later.)

## SCENE 44 — EXT. MID-SEA — THE ANSWER & THE END OF KALAPAHADA — CONTINUOUS
