**Problems still open:**
- **[blocker] Story doctor — Sequence 2, Hero's move / Clue:** The rules explicitly state Soumya activated 'Screen Pinning', disabling touch input and locking the video permanently on screen. Yet, Babulu 'attempts to upload the video to a news app,' which is functionally impossible if he cannot navigate away from the pinned video app. *Fix:* Babulu desperately tries to guess the lock-screen PIN to unpin the app so he can send it, but fails. He then realizes that even if he could unlock it, the cell towers are down, physically trapping the evidence on this specific device.
- **[major] Story doctor — Sequence 1, Ending hook & Sequence 2:** Rudra broadcasts a hyper-specific description of Babulu (yellow shirt, ripped collar, green tote bag). Since Rudra is tracking the IMEI via IMSI catchers to a 50-meter radius, he only knows roughly where the phone is, not who holds it. If his men actually saw Babulu steal it, they would have grabbed him immediately. *Fix:* Rudra's ground team tracks the IMEI to the wealthy tourist. The tourist complains loudly to the plainclothes cops that his green tote bag was just stolen by a street kid in a yellow shirt with a ripped collar, handing Rudra the exact description to broadcast.
- **[major] Story doctor — Sequence 8, Climax:** Babulu throws Rs 50,000 into the air right next to the VVIP barricade, triggering a 'violent frenzy'. In reality, SSB commandos would immediately evacuate the Chief Minister upon seeing a crowd riot form ten feet away, completely ruining the 3-second PR window. *Fix:* Babulu must orchestrate the cash drop in the outer perimeter, directly behind Rudra. The massive crowd surges backward away from the inner ring to grab the money, pinning Rudra against the outer barricades, while leaving the inner VVIP ring completely quiet and safe, allowing the CM to confidently perform his solo PR bow.
- **[major] Story doctor — Sequence 6, Clue:** Babulu uses the 'reflection on a tuba in the police band' to spot cops. A brass police band with tubas would never be inside the terrifying, dense devotee crush of the traditional Pahandi Bije; it contradicts the established physics of the crowd crush. *Fix:* Babulu uses the reflection off a massive, highly polished brass Ghanta (traditional cymbal) being violently played by a nearby traditional Sevayat to spot the plainclothes cops closing in on him.
- **[major] Audience critic — Sequence 5 and Sequence 8:** Babulu retrieves his stash of 50,000 rupees by diving underwater, but later throws the bills into the air 'like confetti'. Wet currency clumps together heavily and cannot flutter or be dispersed easily to create a sprawling frenzy. *Fix:* Have the stash hidden in a dry crevice above the water line at the Markandeswar tank, or firmly sealed inside a watertight plastic jar so the bills remain crisp and easily throwable.
- **[major] Audience critic — Sequence 6 and 7: The Jammer Zone:** The story establishes both IMSI catchers tracking the phone AND a VVIP jammer blocking signals. VVIP jammers block the exact cellular frequencies IMSI catchers rely on. If Babulu is near the CM, the tracking should go completely dark. *Fix:* Turn this into a dramatic escalation. Have Rudra's tracking screen go completely black as Babulu enters the VVIP jammer radius, forcing a panicked Rudra to abandon his watchtower and hunt Babulu purely by sight on the ground.
- **[major] Odisha culture expert — Sequence 6: The Pahandi:** Babulu is described as risking being 'trampled to death under the swaying deities.' Only the Daitapati Sevayats (bare-chested, in traditional silk attire) are allowed in the immediate inner circle carrying the deities. A boy in a yellow shirt getting under the deities would be instantly spotted and violently ejected by the Sevayats and police cordons. *Fix:* Have Babulu navigate the terrifying, breathless crush of the ordinary devotees who are violently pushing against the heavy police rope cordons, desperate to get closer to the deities, rather than being under the deities himself.

## 1. The promise

**Logline:** When a cynical teenage pickpocket steals a phone containing video evidence of a murder, he must outsmart the city's corrupt Superintendent of Police who is using the massive Rath Yatra security grid to hunt him down.

**The question that keeps people watching:** Can a street thief outwit a corrupt SP in a crowd of a million people when the hunter controls the cameras, the exits, and the radio towers?

**What the audience is paying for:** A claustrophobic, high-stakes cat-and-mouse chase thriller amidst the sensory overload of a massive festival.

**Why "wow":** The villain controls the tactical high ground and surveillance grid, forcing the hero to use analog street magic, crowd physics, and ancient festival traditions to survive in plain sight.

## 2. The hidden truth

SP Rudra Pratap runs a massive extortion syndicate skimming funds from VIP temple security. Three days before Rath Yatra, a junior temple servitor named Soumya uncovered the ledgers and recorded Rudra executing a dissenting politician. Cornered on the morning of the festival, Soumya activates 'Screen Pinning' to lock the video, drops the phone in a tourist's bag, and is murdered. Rudra's men hide the body in a police trunk to prevent a disruption of the sacred Niti by the Sevayat Nijogs, but the phone remains in the crowd.

- **Six months ago:** SP Rudra takes charge of Puri and sets up an underground extortion ring disguised as VIP security protocols.
- **Three days before Rath Yatra:** Soumya discovers the hidden ledgers and secretly records Rudra murdering a dissenting local politician in a warehouse.
- **Night before Rath Yatra:** Rudra learns of the leak and dispatches his plainclothes strike team to eliminate Soumya before the Chief Minister arrives.
- **Morning of Rath Yatra:** Soumya is chased into an alley, pins the video to his screen, drops the phone into a tourist's tote bag, and is killed and hidden in a police trunk by Rudra.
- **During the intense morning crowd build-up:** Babulu, working the tourist crowd on Bada Danda, pickpockets the tourist's tote bag, acquiring the locked phone with 90% battery.

**Who knows what at the start:**
- **SP Rudra Pratap:** He killed Soumya and hid the body to prevent a Sevayat Nijog protest, but knows the phone's IMEI is pinging in the crowd.
- **Babulu:** Discovers the locked murder video and realizes the killer's voice matches the man commanding the festival loudspeakers.
- **Inspector Das:** Knows Rudra ordered the hit and knows exactly where Soumya's body is hidden, carrying immense guilt.

## 3. Characters

### Babulu (17) — Protagonist
*Hero. Wiry, sun-burnt, wearing a faded oversized yellow shirt with a ripped left collar to hide stolen goods, quick-eyed and constantly scanning.*

- **Wants:** To save enough stolen money to leave Puri and start a clean life in Mumbai.
- **Needs:** To realize that running away solves nothing, and that he has a responsibility to the city that raised him.
- **Secret:** He has a hidden stash of 50,000 rupees in cash buried under a loose brick in the Markandeswar temple tank.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes everyone is corrupt and looking out only for themselves, so he should do the same.
- **Changes the plot when:** Deduces the exact 3-second VVIP security blind spot by watching a live broadcast of the Chief Minister pushing away SSB guards at the temple gates.
- **Arc:** From a selfish, cynical thief who preys on the crowd, to a localized hero who sacrifices his own freedom and wealth to protect the innocent.

### SP Rudra Pratap (45) — Antagonist
*Antagonist. Impeccably ironed Khaki uniform, aviator sunglasses, imposing posture, always holding a walkie-talkie.*

- **Wants:** To retrieve the phone and eliminate Babulu before the Chief Minister's security detail leaves.
- **Needs:** Total control and submission from the city.
- **Secret:** He despises the religious fervor of the festival and views the million devotees purely as cattle to be managed and monetized.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes his badge makes him untouchable and that street kids are invisible and powerless.
- **Changes the plot when:** Uses IMSI catchers on the watchtowers to track the stolen phone's IMEI to a 50-meter radius, unleashing ruthless ground sweeps.
- **Arc:** From absolute authority commanding the city from above, to public humiliation and physical arrest at ground level.

### Mina (16) — His partner in crime and closest friend
*Ally / Mastermind. Dressed in a bright, cheap Salwar suit, wears fake silver anklets, incredibly fast hands.*

- **Wants:** To keep Babulu safe and stick to their usual small-time thievery.
- **Needs:** To trust that they can punch above their weight and fight the system.
- **Secret:** She deliberately botches some pickpockets if the victim looks genuinely poor.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes survival is the only victory poor people can achieve.
- **Changes the plot when:** Actively orchestrates a flawless decoy trail using a different stolen phone strapped to a cow, pulling cops away, and triggers a smoke distraction underneath the VIP gallery.
- **Arc:** Transforms from a frightened survivor into a brave operative who risks her life and outsmarts the police to breach the VIP cordon.

### Baya Mamu (60) — His mentor
*Mentor. Leathery skin, missing three fingers on his left hand, wears a traditional white dhoti and gamucha.*

- **Wants:** To live out his days in peace running a small festival food stall.
- **Needs:** To pass on his ultimate 'phantom hand' technique for a righteous cause.
- **Secret:** He once tried to rob a politician and lost his fingers as punishment from corrupt cops.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes the system is too big to fall.
- **Changes the plot when:** Teaches Babulu the ultimate reverse-pickpocketing technique while hiding from police, demonstrating that giving is harder than taking.
- **Arc:** Moves from passive cynicism to actively running interference in the street kitchen to buy Babulu time.

### Inspector Das (35) — The conflicted pursuer
*Henchman / Secondary Antagonist. Sweaty, perpetually stressed, plainclothes cop wearing a checkered shirt and jeans, hiding a service weapon.*

- **Wants:** To impress Rudra and secure his own promotion.
- **Needs:** To confront his dwindling morality and actively stop his corrupt boss.
- **Secret:** He knows exactly which police vehicle trunk Soumya's body is rotting in and feels sick with guilt.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes following orders absolves him of guilt.
- **Changes the plot when:** Spots Babulu sneaking under the VIP viewing gallery but intentionally lowers his radio and turns his back, letting him pass.
- **Arc:** Goes from a loyal attack dog to a brave man who ultimately draws his weapon on his own boss to protect the truth.

### Chief Minister Anant Mohanty (55) — The target
*The Unwitting Savior. Crisp white Kurta, surrounded by SSB commandos, polite but exhausted.*

- **Wants:** A flawless, highly publicized PR photo-op at the Rath Yatra showing him as a humble devotee.
- **Needs:** To realize the rot in his own state police.
- **Secret:** He intentionally breaches his own SSB security protocols just to get better solo camera angles.
- **Lie they believe:** Believes his security detail tells him the truth about the city.
- **Changes the plot when:** Steps entirely out of his SSB security bubble to bow barefoot during the VIP Darshan, creating the 3-second physical window Babulu needs.
- **Arc:** Remains functionally the same, but his PR obsession acts as the ultimate blind spot that Rudra cannot control.

**The villain's plan:** SP Rudra plans to use IMSI catchers (Stingrays) on the watchtowers to track the stolen phone's IMEI number to a 50-meter radius. Because local cell networks are down from crowd strain and VVIP jammers block all uploads, Babulu cannot send the video online. Rudra deploys plainclothes strike teams to physically hunt within the pinged radius, intending to quietly assassinate Babulu, retrieve the phone, and write the death off as a stampede casualty. He hid Soumya's body in a trunk to avoid a Sevayat Nijog protest disrupting the sacred Niti, believing his brutal extortion is just a 'tax' for managing the chaotic city.

## 4. Facts sheet

**Jobs and money:**
- Pickpockets operate entirely in cash; Babulu's life savings (50,000 rupees) are entirely in one hundred 500-rupee notes.
- SP Rudra uses untraceable temple donation cash to pay his hitmen.
- The local street vendors pay a protection fee to the police, which Babulu exploits to blend in behind their stalls.

**Health:**
- The extreme July humidity of Puri causes rapid dehydration; running through the dense crowd exhausts stamina quickly.
- Baya Mamu's missing fingers mean he cannot execute complex sleight of hand anymore, forcing Babulu to perform the climax trick.
- The dense crowd crush during the Pahandi makes running impossible; everyone moves as a swaying ocean of bodies.

**Belongings and vehicles:**
- Soumya activated 'Screen Pinning' before dying, locking the video on screen and disabling touch input so it cannot be accidentally muted.
- The phone battery starts at 90 percent and ticks down in real-time throughout the movie, adding a relentless deadline.
- Police vehicles cannot move through Bada Danda; police must move on horseback or in foot-based wedge formations.

**Places:**
- Bada Danda: The three-kilometer Grand Road where the chariots are pulled. A sea of a million people, claustrophobic and loud.
- The Mathas: Ancient, interconnected temple monasteries with unmapped rooftops offering escape from ground crowds.
- The Mancha (VIP Viewing Gallery): A massive, multi-tiered scaffolding structure where VIPs sit securely above the crowd.

**Rules of this world:**
- There is no supernatural magic; survival depends entirely on analog street smarts, crowd physics, and physical sleight of hand.
- Local cell towers completely collapse under data strain; state VVIP jammers create a total dead zone around the Chief Minister.
- IMSI catchers (Stingrays) only provide a 50-meter radius for tracking an IMEI, forcing physical ground hunts by plainclothes cops.
- The Chief Minister uses the ground-level VIP Darshan window before the Gajapati Maharaja's arrival as a PR opportunity, deliberately stepping out of his SSB bubble to appear humble on live TV.

## 5. The climax (designed first)

**Where/when:** The highly secured VVIP cordon next to Nandighosa (Lord Jagannath's chariot) on Bada Danda, exactly during the ground-level VIP Darshan window before the Gajapati Maharaja arrives.

**The reversal:** The audience expects Babulu to try and escape the jammer zone to upload the video. Instead, he runs into the most heavily guarded zone in Puri to reverse-pickpocket the phone onto the Chief Minister.

**The hero's choice:** Babulu chooses to sacrifice his hidden escape fund of 50,000 rupees, untying the bundle and throwing it high into the air like confetti over his network of street kids and beggars. This creates a massive, chaotic physical wall against Rudra, while leaving the VVIP ring perfectly calm.

**The cost:** He permanently loses his ticket to a clean life in Mumbai, trapping himself in the chaotic, dangerous streets of Puri forever.

**Payoffs that fire:**
- Babulu's hidden stash of 50,000 in 500-rupee notes buried under a brick at the Markandeswar temple tank.
- Soumya's 'Screen Pinning' accessibility feature keeping the video active.
- Baya Mamu's lesson on the wrist-flick reverse-pickpocket trick under the table.
- Babulu watching the live broadcast of the CM pushing away his SSB guards for a solo PR bow at the temple gates.
- Inspector Das staring at his holstered weapon, sweating with guilt over hiding Soumya's body in a trunk.

**Final image:** Live on national television, as the massive crowd prepares to pull the chariot ropes, the Chief Minister stands in his viewing area staring at the looping murder video. Below him, Inspector Das holds Rudra at gunpoint, while Babulu vanishes smiling into the roaring ocean of devotees.

**Why "wow":** SP Rudra's inescapable, high-tech digital trap is shattered by analog street magic, a sprawling cash frenzy triggering a beggar network, and the perfect exploitation of a politician's predictable live TV PR stunt.

**After:** Babulu accepts Puri as his home, becoming a local protector. SP Rudra is arrested at gunpoint by his own subordinate. Inspector Das finds his courage and exposes the hidden body. Mina takes over the street network alongside Babulu, and the CM gets his undeniable proof.

## 7. Sequence blueprints

### Sequence 1: The Harvest on Bada Danda

- **Cold open:** At the quiet Markandeswar tank, Babulu hides cash under a brick. Nearby, Soumya pins a murder video on his screen, drops the phone in a tourist's bag, and is murdered. Cops throw his body into a vehicle trunk to prevent a Nijog protest.
- **Question:** Can a teenage street thief survive stealing the most dangerous item in Puri?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu works the intense morning crowd build-up, identifies the wealthy tourist, and flawlessly pickpockets the tote bag to steal the phone.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra realizes the phone is in the crowd, tracks its IMEI via watchtower IMSI catchers, and deploys ground teams.
- **Clue (earned):** Babulu notices blood on the case, sees the pinned video of the murder, and realizes the killer's voice matches the man commanding the festival loudspeakers.
- **False lead:** none
- **Hero learns:** The man commanding the entire festival security is the murderer on the screen.
- **Audience learns:** The Screen Pinning feature means the video cannot be stopped and the 90% battery will drain relentlessly.
- **Set piece:** A rhythmic, high-tension pickpocketing montage synchronized with the morning temple chants and surging devotee crowds.
- **Side story:** Mina is busy creating petty distractions for Babulu, unaware they are stepping into a murder plot.
- **Turn:** Babulu decides not to ditch the phone but to exploit it to fund his Mumbai escape.
- **Ending hook:** Rudra takes over the PA loudspeakers in Babulu's exact sector, calling out a 'suspect in a yellow shirt with a ripped left collar, carrying a green canvas tote bag'. Babulu freezes.
- **Plants:** Babulu's hidden stash of Rs 50,000 in 500-rupee notes buried under a brick at the Markandeswar temple tank.

### Sequence 2: The Dead Zone

- **Cold open:** With his hyper-specific description echoing over the PA system, Babulu sprints into the suffocating crowd as plainclothes hitmen begin physically sweeping his 50-meter radius.
- **Question:** How does a thief escape a hunter who controls the tactical high ground?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu evades the watchtower line-of-sight by stealing a traditional Pipli umbrella and navigating blind spots in the street stalls.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra tightens the IMSI catcher radius on the IMEI, directing his brutal ground teams to physically assault anyone matching Babulu's description.
- **Clue (earned):** Babulu attempts to upload the video to a news app, discovering that the local cell towers have completely collapsed under the crowd's data strain.
- **False lead:** Babulu believes he can slip out through an alley, only to find armored riot police blocking the exits.
- **Hero learns:** There is no digital escape; the internet is useless, and the battery is fiercely draining due to max brightness and network searching.
- **Audience learns:** Rudra's ground teams are ruthless and will kill in plain sight if the crowd provides cover.
- **Set piece:** A claustrophobic cat-and-mouse chase through a vibrant labyrinth of street vendors, with Babulu using his knowledge of protection-racket stalls to hide.
- **Side story:** Mina realizes Babulu is being hunted by cops and decides to intervene, attacking a hitman.
- **Turn:** Babulu realizes he cannot outrun the physical grid; he needs specialized help to counter police tactics.
- **Ending hook:** Mina is briefly snagged by a hitman in an alleyway, forcing Babulu to stop running and formulate a rescue.
- **Plants:** Soumya's phone has 'Screen Pinning' activated; touch input is disabled and the video loops relentlessly, aggressively draining the 90% battery.

### Sequence 3: The Matha Corridors

- **Cold open:** Babulu uses a stolen mirror to blind the hitman with sunlight, allowing Mina to break free, and they dive into the ancient, interconnected temple monasteries.
- **Question:** How to outmaneuver an omnipresent enemy without relying on technology?
- **Hero's move:** Babulu seeks out his retired mentor, Baya Mamu, running a chaotic street-kitchen on the monastery rooftops to stay off the ground.
- **Villain's move:** Rudra, frustrated by the IMSI signal bouncing off temple stone, orders Inspector Das to forcefully interrogate the local street urchins.
- **Clue (earned):** While hiding under a prep table from a passing patrol, Baya Mamu explains that planting an object requires true mastery, teaching the wrist-flick.
- **False lead:** none
- **Hero learns:** The only way to expose Rudra is to physically reverse-pickpocket the phone onto someone with higher authority than the SP.
- **Audience learns:** Baya Mamu lost his fingers to corrupt cops, establishing the extreme physical stakes of failing.
- **Set piece:** A high-stress hiding sequence in a bustling street-kitchen, where the 'put-pocket' lesson occurs inches away from patrolling boots.
- **Side story:** Inspector Das forcefully questions kids, his hand hovering over his holster as he drowns in guilt over the body in his trunk.
- **Turn:** Babulu shifts from fleeing the city to planning an infiltration into the most heavily guarded zone on earth.
- **Ending hook:** Babulu decides the target must be the Chief Minister, realizing the terrifying level of security he must breach.
- **Plants:** Baya Mamu demonstrates a 'put-pocket' trick under a table while hiding from cops, stating giving is harder than taking.

### Sequence 4: The Relay

- **Cold open:** Rudra publicly humiliates Inspector Das for losing Babulu, demanding he scour the slums until the boy is found or lose his badge.
- **Question:** Can the street network outpace the police radio network?

- **Villain's move:** Rudra realizes the IMEI is moving too fast for one person and orders his men to use batons to break the chain of children.
