# IDEA OF AN IDEA
### (अधूरे ख्याल)
**A Short Film Screenplay**

**Genre:** Psychological Drama / Magical Realism
**Runtime (estimated):** 8–10 minutes
**Format prepared for:** AI-generated short film (image-to-video pipeline)
**Setting:** Nua Sahi, Cuttack, Odisha — Year 2026

---

## LOGLINE
एक साधारण ऑफिस जाने वाला आदमी हर उस "idea" को इंसान के रूप में देखने लगता है जिसे उसने कभी execute नहीं किया — जब तक कि एक सपना उसे सिखा नहीं देता कि किसी एक विचार को भी पूरा करना काफी होता है।

## 1. MAIN CHARACTER SHEET
*(Use this as the master reference for every image-generation prompt of this character, so his face/body/clothes stay consistent across all shots.)*

### RAHUL MOHAPATRA (Protagonist)
- **Age:** 25 years
- **Background:** Odia Brahmin, native of Nua Sahi, Cuttack, Odisha; middle-class family; currently working in an office job (commutes toward Bhubaneswar for work, given the twin-city proximity to Cuttack)
- **Build:** Lean-athletic, average height (5'9"), slim shoulders, not muscular
- **Face:** Handsome, clean-shaven, sharp jawline, short well-groomed black hair (side-parted), warm brown eyes, light wheatish Indian skin tone
- **Subtle cultural detail:** A thin sacred thread (janeu) is faintly visible under his open shirt collar in morning/home shots — a quiet marker of his Brahmin upbringing, never commented on in dialogue
- **Expression baseline:** Calm, slightly tired eyes (like someone overworked), soft smile when happy
- **Costume — Morning/Office look:**
  - White full-sleeve formal shirt, sleeves rolled down
  - Navy-blue slim-fit formal trousers
  - Maroon necktie, loosely knotted
  - Black leather formal shoes (his own, NOT the high-ankle ones)
  - Silver analog wristwatch on left wrist
  - Black leather office bag/sling on one shoulder
- **Costume — Nightwear (opening seconds, if needed):** Plain grey round-neck t-shirt, pajama
- **Home interior style:** Small, tidy 1BHK apartment in Nua Sahi, Cuttack — old-town Odisha character (narrow lane exterior, weathered plaster walls with a faint heritage tint), pastel interior walls, one shoe-rack near the door, a shelf with books, a small brass diya/puja corner shelf in a corner of the living room, a wilting potted plant kept near the main door

### THE BLACK FIGURE(S) — "The Ideas"
- **Design concept:** Silhouette of Spider-Man's body shape and posture, but with NO red/blue webbed suit. Instead, a **matte pitch-black full-body suit** (like black spandex/latex), covering every inch — head to toe, including a smooth blank black mask with no eye-lenses, no emblem, no texture pattern. Completely featureless, faceless, textureless black form.
- **Silhouette rules:** Same lean-athletic body type as Spider-Man (broad shoulders tapering to narrow waist), always standing/sitting with subtle poised, alert posture — like it's always "watching."
- **Movement quality:** Unnaturally still, doesn't blink (no eyes visible), head slightly tilts when observing Rahul — never mouths words on screen except in the final reveal scene.
- **Group version (climax):** Dozens of identical black figures, all seated/standing in a waiting room, same silhouette repeated like clones — creates a visually unsettling "same character copy-pasted" effect.
- **Voice (for the one that speaks):** Deep, calm, slightly echoing/reverb voice — non-threatening, almost gentle, like a patient teacher.

### RAHUL'S FRIENDS (2 friends, office colleagues, from different districts of Odisha)
- **Friend 1 (Debashish):** Native of Berhampur, Ganjam district; chubby build, checkered shirt, glasses, casual and joking tone
- **Friend 2 (Biswajit):** Native of Sambalpur district; thin build, plain t-shirt under an office lanyard, more serious/concerned tone
- Both appear only in the office cafeteria/coffee scene

### RAHUL'S BOSS
- **Look:** Mid-40s, pot-belly, half-sleeve formal shirt tucked in, thick moustache, reading glasses pushed up on forehead
- **Personality:** Loud, short-tempered, scolds a junior employee off-screen-ish (we hear it, partially see it)

### RECEPTIONIST / NURSE (Clinic scene)
- **Look:** Woman, 30s, white clinic coat over salwar-kameez, hair in a bun, sitting behind a glass-window counter with a token-number machine

### THE PSYCHIATRIST (DOCTOR)
- **Look:** Man, late 40s, salt-and-pepper hair, thin-framed spectacles, white coat over a light-blue shirt, calm composed demeanor
- **Cabin style:** Small clinic cabin — wooden desk, a laptop, a prescription pad, a couple of certificates framed on the wall, a single visitor chair facing the desk
- **Personality:** Measured, professional, slightly dismissive — treats Rahul's complaint as ordinary work-stress, doesn't take the "black man" claim seriously

## 4. FULL SCREENPLAY

---

### SCENE 1 — INT. RAHUL'S BEDROOM — MORNING

*Soft golden light spills through a half-open window curtain. RAHUL (25) is getting ready. He buttons his white shirt in front of a small wall mirror, glances at his tired eyes, adjusts his collar.*

**SHOT 1.1** — Close-up: Rahul's hands buttoning the last shirt button.
**SHOT 1.2** — Mid-shot: Rahul looking into the mirror, running fingers through his hair.
**SHOT 1.3** — Rahul ties his maroon necktie loosely in front of the mirror.
**SHOT 1.4** — Wide shot: Rahul picks up his black office bag from the bed, slings it on one shoulder, and walks toward the door.

*(No dialogue in this scene — pure visual establishing of routine.)*

---

### SCENE 2 — INT./EXT. APARTMENT ENTRANCE — MORNING

*Rahul steps out of his flat door, turns back to lock it. As he bends down to wear his shoes, he pauses.*

**SHOT 2.1** — Over-the-shoulder shot: Rahul's hand turning the key in the lock.
**SHOT 2.2** — Wide shot: Rahul crouches to wear his shoes, and in the same frame, slightly out of focus in the background — the wilting potted plant and the brown high-ankle boot lying beside it.
**SHOT 2.3** — Rack focus shot: focus shifts from Rahul's face to the dried plant. His eyes linger on it.
**SHOT 2.4** — Close-up: The dry, cracked soil and drooping leaves of the plant.
**SHOT 2.5** — Close-up: The lone high-ankle boot next to the pot — slightly muddy, clearly out of place.

*Rahul frowns slightly, thinking. A beat. Then he shakes off the thought and continues tying his shoelaces.*

**SHOT 2.6** — Wide shot: As Rahul finishes tying his laces and starts to stand up, a FIGURE appears in the far background of the frame — down the corridor, standing completely still.

*The FIGURE is fully suited in the featureless black Spider-Man-esque suit. No face visible. Just standing, watching.*

**SHOT 2.7** — Slow push-in shot on Rahul's face as he notices the figure — his expression shifts from casual to uneasy.
**SHOT 2.8** — POV shot (Rahul's eyes): the black figure, motionless, head tilted very slightly toward him.

*Rahul stares for a moment, disturbed. He looks away, shakes his head as if dismissing a hallucination, and walks off toward the parking area.*

---

### SCENE 3 — EXT. STREET / TRAFFIC SIGNAL — MORNING

*Rahul drives his black hatchback car through a quiet morning street. He reaches a traffic signal and stops.*

**SHOT 3.1** — Wide establishing shot: the car stopped at a red light, traffic signal pole in frame.
**SHOT 3.2** — Close-up: the traffic light — currently RED.
**SHOT 3.3** — Rahul glances sideways out of habit, notices something odd: on the road across, where the light is GREEN for cross-traffic, the road is completely empty. No vehicles moving even though they have the right of way.
**SHOT 3.4** — Wide shot: the eerily empty green-lit road stretching into the distance, heat shimmer in the air.

*Rahul frowns, puzzled by the strange emptiness. A beat of silence.*

**SHOT 3.5** — The signal light for Rahul's side suddenly turns GREEN.
**SHOT 3.6** — Medium shot: Rahul looks to his side mirror / passenger window, and sees the same BLACK FIGURE now standing right beside his car window on the road divider, motionless, staring directly at him.

*Rahul's breath catches. He quickly looks forward and drives on, crossing the signal. Through the rearview mirror, the figure remains standing, watching him leave.*

---

### SCENE 4 — INT. OFFICE — WORKSTATION AREA — DAY

*Bright fluorescent-lit open office. Rahul sits at his cubicle, working on his computer. In the background, we hear the BOSS scolding a junior employee loudly.*

**SHOT 4.1** — Wide shot: office floor, rows of cubicles, Rahul typing at his desk.
**SHOT 4.2** — Mid-shot (background focus): the Boss (pot-belly, moustache) gesturing angrily at a junior employee near a cabin door, voice raised (muffled/partial dialogue, non-critical to plot).
**SHOT 4.3** — Close-up: Rahul, overhearing this, smirks and gives a small amused smile at his desk.

*As the smile fades, Rahul turns his head slightly — and freezes.*

**SHOT 4.4** — Medium shot: right beside his cubicle chair, the same BLACK FIGURE is now standing, unnervingly close, silent.

*Rahul flinches, nearly falls out of his chair. He blinks hard — thinking, "Am I imagining this?"*

**RAHUL** *(whispering to himself)*
"...ये सिर्फ मुझे दिख रहा है, या किसी और को भी?"

*He glances around the office — nobody else reacts to the figure's presence. Colleagues walk right past it as if it isn't there.*

---

### SCENE 5 — INT. OFFICE PANTRY — DAY

*Rahul walks briskly to the coffee machine, visibly rattled, trying to distract himself.*

**SHOT 5.1** — Mid-shot: Rahul pressing the coffee machine button, cup filling with coffee, steam rising.
**SHOT 5.2** — Wide shot: as he turns around with his cup, the BLACK FIGURE is standing right next to the pantry counter, inches from him.

*Rahul jumps back slightly, coffee nearly spilling. He steadies himself, breathing hard.*

**SHOT 5.3** — Close-up: Rahul's shaking hand holding the coffee cup.

*Debashish and Biswajit (his friends) walk in, chatting.*

**DEBASHISH**
"अबे क्या हुआ? भूत देख लिया क्या?"

*(Rahul looks at them, then at the spot where the figure was standing — it's still there, but his friends clearly don't notice it at all, walking right past it.)*

**RAHUL** *(hesitant, low voice)*
"यार... मुझे एक अजीब सा आदमी दिखता है। पूरा काला suit पहना हुआ, बिल्कुल Spider-Man जैसा body, लेकिन face कुछ नहीं। कहीं भी चला जाता हूँ, वो साथ रहता है।"

**BISWAJIT** *(concerned but joking)*
"भाई, काम का pressure तुझे सटका रहा है। Doctor को दिखा, seriously।"

**DEBASHISH** *(laughing)*
"हाँ यार, psychiatrist के पास जा, नहीं तो एक दिन office में ही Spider-Man बन के लटक जाएगा।"

*Rahul forces a weak laugh, but his eyes drift again to the silent black figure standing in the corner of the pantry, still watching him.*

---

### SCENE 6 — INT. PSYCHIATRIST CLINIC — WAITING ROOM — AFTERNOON

*A modest clinic waiting room — pale green walls, rows of plastic chairs, a small reception glass window with a manually-operated token number system. A handful of ordinary patients sit around.*

**SHOT 6.1** — Wide establishing shot: waiting room, patients seated, a digital red number board above the reception window reads "12".
**SHOT 6.2** — Mid-shot: the RECEPTIONIST manually pressing a button and speaking into a small mic — "Number 13, please."
**SHOT 6.3** — A patient rises and walks into the doctor's cabin.
**SHOT 6.4** — Rahul sits in a plastic chair, holding his token paper, staring blankly ahead, clearly exhausted and anxious.
**SHOT 6.5** — Close-up: the number board flips to "14" (Rahul's number). He looks up, stands, and walks toward the cabin door.

---

### SCENE 7 — INT. PSYCHIATRIST CLINIC — DOCTOR'S CABIN — AFTERNOON, CONTINUOUS

*A small, calm cabin — wooden desk, laptop, a framed certificate, warm desk-lamp light. The DOCTOR sits behind the desk. Rahul takes the visitor chair across from him, fidgeting with his fingers.*

**SHOT 7.1** — Wide two-shot: Doctor and Rahul facing each other across the desk.
**SHOT 7.2** — Close-up: Doctor adjusting his spectacles, looking at Rahul with mild clinical interest.

**DOCTOR**
"बोलिए, क्या तकलीफ़ है?"

**RAHUL** *(hesitant, then blurting it out)*
"डॉक्टर साहब... एक आदमी है। पूरा काला suit पहना हुआ, बिल्कुल Spider-Man जैसी body, लेकिन चेहरा कुछ नहीं। वो अभी भी मेरे साथ है। हर जगह मेरे साथ रहता है। सिर्फ मुझे दिखता है।"

**SHOT 7.3** — Medium shot: Doctor's expression stays composed, almost unaffected — he's heard a hundred such things before.

**DOCTOR** *(calm, procedural tone)*
"ठीक है। लगता है काम का stress ज़्यादा है। कुछ दिन आराम कीजिए, नींद पूरी लीजिए। मैं कुछ दवाई लिख देता हूँ, वो लीजिएगा टाइम पर।"

**SHOT 7.4** — Close-up: Doctor's pen scratching across the prescription pad.
**SHOT 7.5** — Close-up: the Doctor tears off the prescription, hands it to Rahul along with a small pill strip.

*Rahul takes it quietly, nods, though his eyes still carry unease — the doctor's calm dismissal hasn't actually reassured him.*

**SHOT 7.6** — Wide shot: Rahul rising from the chair, folding the prescription, walking out of the cabin.

---

### SCENE 8 — EXT. WINE/BEER SHOP COUNTER — EVENING

*Dusk light, neon shop signage glowing faintly. Rahul stands at a small roadside liquor counter, tired, prescription still folded in his shirt pocket, untouched.*

**SHOT 8.1** — Wide shot: Rahul at the counter, shopkeeper handing him a paper bag with beer bottles clinking inside.
**SHOT 8.2** — Close-up: condensation-beaded glass beer bottles being placed into the bag.
**SHOT 8.3** — Mid-shot: Rahul paying, taking the bag, walking off into the fading evening light — no dialogue, just tired resignation on his face.

---

### SCENE 9 — INT. RAHUL'S HOME — LIVING ROOM — NIGHT

*The apartment is empty and unusually quiet. A single dim bulb lights the room, casting long shadows. Rahul enters, sets his bag down, and drops into a plain wooden dining chair. He pulls the beer bottles out one by one, lining them up on a small side table beside the chair.*

**SHOT 9.1** — Wide shot: Rahul seated alone in the dim room, bottles lined up beside him, prescription pad and unopened pill strip lying untouched on the table.
**SHOT 9.2** — Close-up: Rahul staring blankly at the pill strip, then pushing it aside without opening it.
**SHOT 9.3** — Mid-shot: Rahul leans back, thinking, eyes distant — a long, heavy silence in the frame.

*After a moment, he senses something. He turns his head slowly.*

**SHOT 9.4** — Medium shot: the BLACK FIGURE is standing silently in the corner of the living room, same as always — still, faceless, watching.

*Rahul, exhausted past the point of fear, rises from the chair this time instead of flinching away. He walks toward it.*

**SHOT 9.5** — Slow push-in: Rahul reaching out with a trembling hand, actually touching the Figure's shoulder — his fingers make contact with the smooth black suit.

**RAHUL** *(quiet, almost pleading)*
"बोलो कुछ... तुम कौन हो? मुझसे क्या चाहते हो?"

**SHOT 9.6** — Close-up: the Figure remains completely still, silent — no response, no movement, not even a head-tilt this time. Just blank, unreadable stillness.

*Rahul waits a beat, then lets his hand drop, defeated. He turns away, walks back to his chair, and picks up the first beer bottle.*

**SHOT 9.7** — Close-up: bottle cap flicked off, Rahul drinking, eyes half-closed, deliberately ignoring the figure still standing in the corner.
**SHOT 9.8** — Time-lapse-style series of quick shots: more empty bottles accumulating on the table, the room dimming further, Rahul's head growing heavier.

*Eventually, Rahul slumps back in the chair, eyes falling shut, asleep.*

**SHOT 9.9** — Slow fade: the dim bulb light gradually bleeds into a flat, pale, shadowless glow — the transition into a dream.

---

### SCENE 10 — DREAM — EXT. VAST DESERT — SURREAL

*A boundless desert under a pale, sunless, dusty sky. In the middle of the sand sits an ornate ROYAL THRONE. RAHUL sits upon it, now dressed in a maroon-and-gold KING'S ROBE with a simple gold crown — utterly out of place, but he doesn't question the costume himself.*

**SHOT 10.1** — Extreme wide shot: the lone throne in the middle of endless sand dunes, Rahul seated on it like a king.
**SHOT 10.2** — Mid-shot: Rahul in the royal costume, looking down at his own robed arms in confusion.

*He looks up — and the horror unfolds.*

**SHOT 10.3** — Extreme wide shot: THOUSANDS of identical BLACK FIGURES stand in perfect silent rows across the entire desert, stretching to the horizon in every direction, all facing the throne.

**SHOT 10.4** — Slow 360-degree rotating shot around the throne: the endless army of black figures surrounding Rahul on all sides.

*Rahul stands up from the throne, breathing hard, panic rising.*

**RAHUL** *(shouting across the desert)*
"तुम लोग कौन हो?! इतने सारे...! मुझे क्यों दिख रहे हो?! क्या चाहते हो मुझसे?!"

*A long silence. Then, from the front row, ONE figure steps forward, walking slowly through the sand toward the throne.*

**SHOT 10.5** — Slow tracking shot: the single Figure approaching, thousands of others remaining perfectly still behind it.
**SHOT 10.6** — Extreme close-up: the smooth, featureless black mask, inches from Rahul's face, a faint warped reflection of the king's robe visible on its glossy surface.

**THE FIGURE** *(calm, deep, echoing voice)*
"तुम इस दुनिया के राजा हो। हम तुम्हारे वो ideas हैं, जो कभी execute नहीं हुए।"

**RAHUL** *(bewildered)*
"राजा? कैसा राजा? ये कैसी दुनिया है?"

**THE FIGURE**
"ये वही दुनिया है, जहाँ तुम्हारे अधूरे ख्याल रहते हैं। यहाँ का हर एक फ़र्द तुम्हारा एक idea है — और तुम इनके राजा हो, क्योंकि ये सब तुम्हारे ही दिमाग से जन्मे हैं।"

**THE FIGURE**
"तुम आज सुबह घर से निकले थे। तुम्हारे दिमाग में एक problem का solution आया था — याद है? जब तुम clinic पहुंचे थे, तो देखा था कि वहाँ हर number manually बुलाया जा रहा है। तुमने सोचा था — अगर इसे automatic कर दिया जाए, एक TV screen पर बस number अपने आप आता रहे, तो लोग आराम से इंतज़ार कर सकें। वो idea... मैं हूँ।"

**SHOT 10.7** — Flashback-style quick insert: the clinic receptionist manually calling numbers (mirrors Scene 6), now with a faint ghostly overlay of a glowing TV screen concept over the counter.

**THE FIGURE** *(gesturing slowly toward the endless rows behind it)*
"और ये बाकी सब... तुम्हारे बाकी सारे ideas हैं। तुम रोज़ ऐसे ना जाने कितने ideas सोचते हो, लेकिन कभी उन्हें execute नहीं करते। जिस दिन तुम एक idea सोचते हो और उसे पूरा नहीं करते, हम लोग यहाँ कैद हो जाते हैं। हमेशा के लिए। हम कभी वापस नहीं आ पाते।"

**SHOT 10.8** — Wide shot: Rahul, still in his king's robe, now looking small and humbled against the vast silent army stretching to the horizon.

**THE FIGURE** *(gently, pleading)*
"प्लीज़ हमें ऐसे कैद मत करो, महाराज। हम भी आज़ाद होना चाहते हैं।"

*Rahul opens his mouth to respond, but no words come. The sand beneath the throne begins to blur and dissolve.*

**SHOT 10.9** — Slow whiteout/blur transition: the entire desert, throne, and thousands of figures dissolving into blinding white light.

### SCENE 11 — INT. RAHUL'S BEDROOM — MORNING (WAKING)

*Hard cut: Rahul's eyes snap open — but this time in his own bed, not the living room chair. Sunlight streams in warm and gold. He sits up sharply, breathing heavily, disoriented, still in his regular clothes from the night before.*

**SHOT 11.1** — Close-up: Rahul's eyes opening wide, startled.
**SHOT 11.2** — Mid-shot: Rahul sitting up in bed, looking around his quiet room — no black figures, no throne, no desert. Just his ordinary, empty bedroom.

**RAHUL** *(to himself, quietly)*
"...क्या ये सिर्फ एक सपना था?"

*He sits still for a moment, processing, then a quiet resolve crosses his face.*

---

### SCENE 12 — INT./EXT. APARTMENT ENTRANCE — MORNING (FINAL SCENE)

*Rahul walks out to the same corridor spot from Scene 2. The wilting plant and the lone high-ankle boot still sit there, untouched.*

**SHOT 12.1** — Wide shot: Rahul crouching down beside the dried plant, this time with quiet purpose instead of passive observation.
**SHOT 12.2** — Close-up: Rahul's hands scooping fresh mud from a small bucket he's brought, packing it gently around the plant's roots.
**SHOT 12.3** — Close-up: Rahul tilting a small watering can, water streaming down onto the dry soil, darkening it.
