# 3. THE CHARACTERS (and their arcs)

Each hero had a wound, then became the OPPOSITE of their element to hide from it. Their arc = opening that element again.

**RUDRA — Fire. THE MAIN HERO.** Wound: the Martian war, war droids, nearly lost his sister. Now: over-careful engineer, terrified of his own fire, won't make flame. His cautious over-push causes the breach. Arc: learn to burn / let go. At the climax he gives his life, is forged by the lathi into a warrior, and becomes its bearer. Voice: measured, careful; later fierce.

**MEERA — Water.** Wound: saved a crewmate, lost key data, decided she was "wrong." Now: cold strategist who runs numbers on human lives. Arc: learn what can't be counted; she cracks the winning idea and answers Kalapahada. Voice: exact, cold; later warm.

**OMM — Earth. Heart + humour.** Wound: hid behind rules once, people got hurt. Now: warm cook who knows every name, a healer. Arc: learn to put a load down / let others help. Wounded in the Akhada; at the climax he yields his timing and pulls Tara back. Voice: warm, funny, plain.

**SAMIR — Air. Youngest.** Wound: ran from a wreckage, hurt his family. Now: grave, never jokes, sends all his money to them; sometimes FREEZES at key moments. Arc: stop trying to buy forgiveness; he gives away his chip; at the climax he holds the gap he once froze at. Voice: terse, grave.

**TARA — Ether/space. The sceptic.** Wound: a rift fractured her mind; she now hates mysticism and treats her visions as a symptom. Now: blunt, data-first. Powers: portals; she is the conduit for the final strike; her weakness = losing her grip on the present. Home: Granthaloka; her father dies. Arc: accept a truth she can't prove. Voice: blunt, dry.

**THE RAKHYAKA — old guardian/mentor.** Trains them; dies at the interval; his spirit returns once at the climax. Only he (and Kalapahada, by force) can hold the lathi.

**KALAPAHADA — the villain.** Once a chosen protector; life broke him; he came to hate the Lord; refused the cycle; reincarnates keeping his memories. Wants the Brahma → portal → Samudra Manthan → immortality. Holds the lathi by force. Conquers planets to build an army and waits for the Lord to return. His cry: "I gave everything, I got nothing." Guardrail: he never mocks devotion itself — only his own lost access to it. Keep his NAME secret until Granthaloka.

# 4. THE WORLD & LORE (for the database)

**Setting:** 5000 AD. Prayer is a crime across the galaxy; Earth is the last protected holy place ("God's Planet"). Futuristic Puri: the Grand Road, Sri Mandir, Gundicha Temple, the Wireless Orbital Receiver, the three chariots.

**Powers & the fight:** elements come from the hands (fire/water/earth/air/ether). Kalapahada ABSORBS any elemental attack and grows stronger (him only, not his army). The counter: don't hit him — let force pass through (yield). The final move: one thread, each power into the next, through Tara.

**The lathi (danda):** the holy staff, made of neem wood (nimba daru) of the Lord. It serves ONLY a heart that owes nothing; its power KILLS the unworthy who touch it; only dark power can force it. Kalapahada chains it by force. Rudra earns it at the climax by sacrifice. (This time the danda chose FIVE protectors, not one — a first ever.)

**The Akhada (void):** a timeless world (no time, no aging), made from the neem-wood danda. The portal opens only by a spell + striking the lathi. Time-freeze: the guardian's spell freezes all Earth-time; it is spent after one use (can't be reused).

**Zombify:** shadow-soldiers don't kill — they touch a human and turn them into another shadow-soldier. This is how the army grows.

**The Brahma:** the sacred life-substance inside the Lord's idol. Kalapahada needs it for the Manthan. Must be recovered and restored for Niladri Bije.

**The 9-day clock:** the Rath Yatra ritual forces the Lord back to Puri to enter the temple (Bahuda Yatra → Niladri Bije) within ~9 days. Kalapahada waits at the temple. This is the ticking clock.

**The Gopana:** carrying the Lord off-world = a futuristic version of the real historical hiding of the deities. Frame it as GRACE (blessing the oppressed), not fleeing.

**KEY NAMES / REAL TEXTS (use for authentic flavor):**
- **Biswabasu clan** — the tribal devotees / servitors (Daitapati). **Nila Madhava** — the Lord's early jungle form. **King Indradyumna** — the king in the origin myth.
- **Madala Panji** — the REAL chronicle of the Jagannath temple. In the film it appears as a hologram in the Granthaloka library and reveals Kalapahada's history. (The real PDF is in the project: `madala-panji-1940.pdf` — scanned/image, needs OCR.)
- **Malika** — the REAL Odia prophecy verses. Tara's family palm-leaf prophecy is a Malika verse. (Real PDF in the project: `odia-malika-bachana-book.pdf`.)
- **Granthaloka** = Tara's library planet. **Parmarth 1** = Samir's ship. **Krouloka** = Kalapahada's fortress world (placeholder).

**Controversy guardrails:** keep the 9-day timeline; the Lord blesses, never fights; the journey is grace, not flight; Earth stays the special Tirtha; Kalapahada never mocks devotion itself.
